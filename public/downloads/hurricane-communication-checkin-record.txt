A sent or delivered indicator does not confirm human understanding,
a pickup agreement or a completed relay. Texts can fail too.
A missed check-in alone is not proof that rescue is needed. Do not
drive into hazardous conditions to investigate or find a signal.
For known immediate danger, contact emergency services and provide
the last known exact location and needs. Keep private information
limited to trusted people who need it; protect document backups.
