FICTIONAL CHECK (not a business budget or a funding prediction)
Starting cash $6,000 minus payroll $2,400, rent $1,800 and supplier $900
leaves a projected $900 after all three listed payments, before other
costs. With only payroll actually paid, cash is $3,600 and $2,700 of
listed payments remain planned. A requested $5,000 advance stays in the
pending record; it is not added to either balance until available.
Replace the exercise with actual dates and verified payment statuses.
