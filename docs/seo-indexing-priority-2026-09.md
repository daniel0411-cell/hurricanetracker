## Operating rules

- Request indexing in batches of 10–15 URLs, never the entire sitemap.
- Confirm each URL returns 200, has a self-consistent canonical, and is linked from an indexed or high-impression page.
- Record submission date and review GSC coverage after 7 and 14 days.
- Do not submit sitemap URLs as content pages; they are discovery files.
- Do not create synonym pages for `hurricane tracker`, `hurricane map`, or `live hurricane map`.
