## Findings and remedies

### P1: Fictional staffing and verification presented as actual work

The owner confirmed that the three research teams and specialist titles were generated by Antigravity and that no actual teams exist.

- src/pages/about.astro:51 describes a dedicated group of meteorological specialists, GIS analysts, and emergency management researchers.
- src/data/authors.ts:11 defines three purported desks/boards with research responsibilities and professional verification protocols.
- src/pages/blog/[slug].astro:123 unconditionally labels articles as fact-checked by the assigned organization; the same assignment is used in structured author data.
- src/components/AuthorCard.astro:28 unconditionally claims compliance and cross-reference with NOAA, NHC, and FEMA documentation. The rendered pet article shows this claim despite its listed reference being Ready.gov/pets.
- The About rewrite removes the previous explanation of the independent publisher, AI assistance, limited source checks, and professional-review limitations, while adding universal verification and routine audit claims.

Remedy: use the truthful HurricaneHub publisher identity, remove imaginary organizations and credentials from visible content and structured data, and restore accurate process/limitation disclosures. Show source-check scope and date only where actual records support them. Category assignment must not imply a professional review occurred. These changes address demonstrable accuracy issues; they cannot guarantee an AdSense outcome.

### P1: Unavailable storm data rendered as a calm basin

src/components/Homepage.astro:33 turns unavailable or invalid storm data into an empty Atlantic array. The new block at line 132 checks only that array's length, then announces a calm basin and no active named storms.

Observed in the built local worker: the same page simultaneously displays "Atlantic status unavailable" / "This is not a zero-storm result" and "Basin Calm" / "No Active Named Storms in the Atlantic Basin".

Remedy: require stormsAvailable before displaying any empty-feed status, retain the validated feed timestamp, and describe only the returned feed's scope. An empty active-system list does not establish that disturbances, surf, flooding, or local hazards are absent. Provide generic preparedness content without a calm-status claim when data is unavailable. Cover unavailable, stale, validated-empty, and active-system rendered states.

### P2: Category identity mismatch assigns all posts to the fallback board

src/pages/blog/[slug].astro:23 passes getCategory()'s display name to getAuthorForCategory(), which compares slug values at src/data/authors.ts:50.

Structured execution of the current TypeScript modules found 46 posts and five display-name categories; all 46 resolve to editorial-board. Thus the intended specialist attribution does not operate even on its own terms.

Remedy: remove the fictional attribution mechanism first. If real contributor records are introduced later, use explicit records and a shared typed category identity; do not infer review completion from a category.

### P2: Tool promotion claims capabilities the tool does not provide

src/components/Homepage.astro:142 and src/pages/search-index.json.ts:71 claim wind-loss reduction and premium-credit estimation from structural features. The destination calculator instead performs arithmetic using the user's wind premium and an insurer-confirmed additional discount; it does not calculate engineering loss reductions or establish feature-based insurance credits (tool lines 55, 87, 166).

Remedy: describe the tool consistently as comparing a savings scenario using confirmed figures. Keep feature assessment, discount eligibility, and physical loss modeling separate from its actual arithmetic.

### P2: Insurance waiting-period guidance omits material exceptions

src/components/Homepage.astro:147 presents the NFIP 30-day waiting period as universal. Official FloodSmart guidance lists exceptions, including certain mortgage transactions and other qualifying circumstances.

Remedy: say NFIP coverage generally has a 30-day waiting period, exceptions apply, and the policyholder should confirm the actual effective date with the agent. Replace the nearby unsupported generic "bridge departure checkpoints" wording with checking current local bridge restrictions and evacuation instructions.
